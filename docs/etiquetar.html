CRATER GROUND TRUTH

How to label
Mark every crater you can see in this 250 × 250 m HiRISE tile, even small ones.
1. Press on the center of a crater.
2. Drag to its rim and release.
Click inside a circle with Shift to delete it. Z undoes the last one, N goes to the next tile.
Skip things you are not sure about. Do not look at the app's crater layer while labeling.

Progress is saved in this browser. When you finish all tiles, download the file and save it as docs/data/zonas/<zone>/verdad_crateres.json, then run python scripts/evaluar_crateres.py --zona <zone>.
